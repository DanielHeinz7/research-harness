title: The view shows each entry's seal status and a broken log, and review lists every DECISION that does not verify
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

The view never read the seal verdicts that `GET /api/state` already carried, so a forged `framing
chosen` or accept entry in `decisions.md` (a hand edit, a merge, a raw write) looked exactly like
the researcher's own, Explained and Why lines included. `review` also left such an entry out of
"Unverified decisions": it skipped every DECISION whose ref was not `<n.m> accepted`.

Each History card now names its seal status in the quiet meta line (verified, broken, legacy (no
seal), unverifiable here). A DECISION that does not verify also carries a line that says so in
words, "Does not verify: ...", next to a warning sign, so the mark does not depend on colour. When
the ledger is broken, the notice banner at the top names the entry where it breaks and links to it.

`review` lists every DECISION that does not verify, framing choices included. The filter on
`<n.m> accepted` now applies only to the stale and cover bookkeeping, where it belongs.
