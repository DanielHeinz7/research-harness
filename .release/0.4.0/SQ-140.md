---
ref: SQ-140
title: A variant lens can no longer put a line break into the sealed entry
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

A variant canvas is the model's to write, and one written directly can carry any frontmatter:
`canvas.js` decodes a double-quoted scalar with `JSON.parse`, so `lens: "status\nplanted line"`
becomes a real newline. `choose` put such a lens into the sealed DECISION twice — as the chosen
framing's own lens and as every other variant named in the default `Rejected` list — and a second
line there is read back as part of the entry. A plain second line landed in the record as its own
line, and a lens holding a field label such as `**Explained:**` made the choice refuse until the
researcher passed `--rejected` by hand.

Every control character and run of spaces in a lens now collapses to one space before either use,
so a lens is always one line. A planted lens is shown, never refused: it must not block the
researcher's choice. `init --variant` already refuses a lens with a line break, and nothing about
the seal, the parser or an existing log changes.
