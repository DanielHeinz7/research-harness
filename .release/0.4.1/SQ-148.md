---
ref: SQ-148
title: A sed label ends at `;`, so the write after it is parsed
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

`sedScriptWrites` kept `b`, `t`, `T` and `:` among the commands whose argument runs to the end of
the line, so a label swallowed everything after it: `sed -n ':a;w research/canvas.md' copy.md`,
`sed -n '$!ba;w research/canvas.md' copy.md` and `sed -n 'tx;w research/decisions.md' copy.md` were
read as scripts that write nothing, and the guard allowed them.

A label now ends at `;` or a newline, via `sedLabelEnd`, and only `a`, `i`, `c`, `r`, `R` and `#`
still read to the end of the line. GNU sed ends a label at the `;` and BSD sed reads the `;` into
the label only to fail with an undefined label, so ending it here is the stricter reading on both.
The three writes are refused, the ordinary `sed -n ':a;N;$!ba;s/\n/ /g;p' research/canvas.md` stays
a read, and the perimeter probe covers the row as M40.
