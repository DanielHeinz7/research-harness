---
title: Accepting from the canvas works, and the reviewer stops chasing a hash
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

The canvas skill told the researcher to run `/research-harness:accept 3.1`,
which the command has refused since every acceptance needs your explanation. It
now shows `--explained "<your sentence>"`, says the command refuses without
that sentence, and says the sentence is the researcher's own — the model does
not draft it.

The record-review skill asked the reviewer to cite the text hash of a current
statement, but neither `review` nor `status` prints one. It now cites a
statement by its question id, its marker and its exact words, and cites the
hash the log records for an acceptance, where the log has one. A statement that
changed after acceptance is reported as the review reports it; the reviewer
never computes or invents a hash.
