---
title: damaged local sealing keys refuse writes
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

An empty or malformed local `seal.key` could seal new entries with an empty
or shortened key. The harness now requires 64 hex characters before decoding
that key, just as it does for a released key file. Writes are refused when
the key is damaged; the key is never replaced or rewritten. Valid keys,
including those with a trailing newline, and missing-key behavior are unchanged.
