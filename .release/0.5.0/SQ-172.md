---
ref: SQ-172
title: Review reports findings logged before the hypotheses
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

Under a profile that declares the `hypotheses-before-data` check,
`research-harness review` gains a "Findings before hypotheses" section. It
reports every case where the first log entry on element 7 (Findings) comes
before any acceptance on element 4 (Hypotheses) or element 6 (Design), and
stays silent otherwise. It reports and never blocks: a finding logged early,
such as a pilot, is the researcher's to explain
(`references/preregistration.md`).
