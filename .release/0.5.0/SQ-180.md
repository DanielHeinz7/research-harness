---
ref: SQ-180
title: The agenda before a session comes from one place
bump: minor
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

`research-harness agenda [--out <file>]` prints the "To talk about" tab as
Markdown before a session: every field that has a finding and is not yet
accepted, with its findings and their `By` lines, then every question nobody
has answered. `--out` writes exactly that file.

The tab is rendered from the same data. `/api/state` carries the agenda and
the nine human questions, and the view holds no copy of the wording or the
rules, so the CLI export and the tab cannot drift apart. The poster chip on a
field now counts the findings the agenda lists for it.
