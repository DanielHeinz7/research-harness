title: Check literature-note quotations against the claimed printed page
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

A new `scripts/verify-quotes.py` checks a literature note's quotations against one paper's `full.txt` extraction and the printed page the note claims. Only that page is searched, and wording is compared as a contiguous sequence of words after typographic folding (typographic quotes and dashes, the `ﬁ` ligature, case) — never as a substring and never with word boundaries squashed, so `café` stays distinct from `cafe` and `art` is not `cart`. A passage from another page, one invented word, a reversed run, an unmapped page and page zero fail. Quotation forms the gate cannot read word for word are inventoried and rejected for manual inspection instead of being ignored: a blockquote outside the `Quotations` section, a blockquote that is not `> "..." (p. N)`, an inline quotation in prose, an omitted passage (an ellipsis) and a page range (`pp.`); a note with nothing to check reports `not checked: no quotations` and does not pass. The `literature-note/quote-check@1` report records the inventory and each quotation's line, claimed page, physical page and reason, binds the result to the note, PDF, extraction and page-map hashes, prints and stores the wording-only disclaimer, and `--verify` exits 5 when any of those bytes changed since the check. `references/literature-note.md` documents the CLI, grammar, exit codes and report schema.
