title: Session view rebuilt as a galley proof
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

The session view is now a typeset proof. What the authors said or decided is set in one numbered
column, and what an agent proposes waits in the margin as a query tied to its place in the column
by a leader line until the researcher accepts it with a sentence of their own. Field chips carry
each field's number and name and grow with how full the field is, a statement can be linked by its
number (`#st-3.1`), a statement changed since the last read carries a "New" mark, and Present
shows one field and its queries at projector scale. Source Serif 4 and Hanken Grotesk ship with the
plugin; the session server serves them from `/fonts/`. The poster no longer prints on one A4
page: it prints as a two-column proof.
