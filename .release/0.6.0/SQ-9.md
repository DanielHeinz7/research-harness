A design note selects a separate `research/glossary.md`, linked from canvas
and manuscript statements. It specifies researcher-only acceptance with
sealed text-hash cover, source or own-definition provenance, and declared
construct coverage. Mechanical checks cannot certify distinctions, study fit
or measurement validity. The note addresses late qualitative definitions and
lists four implementation work packages with focused verification. This is
design documentation; no glossary command or runtime behavior ships yet.
