Adds `dashboard/variants/canvas-a.html`, a restyled copy of the session view in the visual language of the metacognition pilot's canvas figure: a document masthead, thin-bordered grid cards with mono eyebrow numbers and counts, dashed blanks with italic captions for open/empty elements, one accent on the most-depended-on open element, and marker/seal badges distinguishable by border style, not color alone. Same JS behaviour as `dashboard/canvas.html` (code exchange, in-memory token, `expectedHash` on every write, fixture mode, escaping); `dashboard/canvas.html` itself is unchanged. Offline, responsive from 390px to 1440px, and prints to A4 with a landscape grid page. A design variant only — not wired into `research-harness serve`.
