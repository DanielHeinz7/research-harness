A full, realistic canvas fixture reconstructing a published paper — Gerlach, J. P., &
Cenfetelli, R. T. (2020). *Constant Checking Is Not Addiction: A Grounded Theory of
IT-Mediated State-Tracking*, MIS Quarterly 44(4), 1705-1732 — so the session view (US-2) can be
designed against real content rather than an invented one. `test/fixtures/constant-checking/`
carries a canvas with all nine elements and their 27 questions filled from the paper (two left
`_(empty)_` where the paper genuinely does not answer), each statement paraphrased with a
`src: gerlach-cenfetelli-2020 p. <page>` tag and marked `[stated]` for the paper's own claims or
`[proposed]` for reconstructions, plus five unsealed `DISCOVERY` entries of the kind
`canvas-review` would raise. `test/fixtures/constant-checking-state.json` is the exact
`GET /api/state` response for this project, generated from the real `viewState` code path in
`bin/canvas-serve.js`, not hand-written. `test/fixtures/README.md` is new and documents both
fixture projects.
