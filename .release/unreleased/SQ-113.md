---
ref: SQ-113
title: canvas-review logs findings as open questions, not verdicts
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

`canvas-review`'s DISCOVERY findings used to read like a verdict: "Element 5
reports a sample drawn mainly from the authors' networks; generalizability
claim in 9.1 is not supported." A researcher reading that back has to decode
which field it means before they can talk about it.

The skill now writes each finding as an open question the author team can sit
down with, naming the field by its human question — "Where do we look?",
"Where does it stop?" — instead of "element N" or a statement number. The
`ref` field of the log entry still carries the machine reference. What the
review checks is unchanged: only the wording rule and its example changed.
