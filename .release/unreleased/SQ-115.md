---
ref: SQ-115
title: `init --title` writes the paper's title into the canvas frontmatter
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

`research-harness init` takes an optional `--title <text>`. On a new project it
writes `title: "<text>"` into the canvas frontmatter, on the line after
`project:` and quoted the way `project` and `outlet` already are, so a title
holding a colon or a quote survives a real YAML reader. The session view shows
that title in the header; without `--title` no title line is written at all and
the view falls back to the titleized project slug.

A canvas that already exists is never rewritten, so `init` on an existing
project keeps its rule: given `--title` it prints one line saying the title was
not changed and must be edited by hand in the frontmatter, and exits exactly as
before.
