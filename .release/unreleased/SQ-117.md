---
ref: SQ-117
title: Session view shows a "Why" as a quiet line, not as raw asterisks
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

In the session view, a decision or a note that carries a why no longer shows `**Why:**` or the raw `[accepted, src: …]` marker. History, To talk about and the field view split an entry into its text (marker dropped, page as a superscript) and a quiet second line in the muted colour, for example "Why: Dadadas". `**Rejected:**` and `**Supersedes:**` lines read the same way. The log files are unchanged.
