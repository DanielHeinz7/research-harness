An acceptance is now bound to the text it was given for. `hooks/lib/hash.js`
reduces a statement to eight hex characters of a SHA-256 over its normalized
text, so reflowing a paragraph leaves an acceptance intact while any change of
wording invalidates it. The mechanism is borrowed from the design-mining
artifact, which keys recorded human reviews to a hash of the fragment text so
they survive re-mining.

`hooks/lib/decisions.js` reads and writes `research/decisions.md`. It parses
entry headings for their sequence number, date, kind, reference and optional
`text <hash>` suffix, formats new entries in that grammar, and appends them.
Appending is the only write it performs: history is never rewritten, and a
correction is a new entry naming the one it supersedes.
