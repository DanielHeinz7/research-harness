The plugin now carries its own question set. `references/canvas-elements.md`
holds the nine canvas elements with their numbered questions and is both what
the skills read and the template `research-harness init` copies into a paper
project. `references/amj-map.md` says which part of the *Publishing in AMJ*
series to read for which element. `references/problematization.md` is the
module attached to elements 1 and 2: four sensibilities asked of the material
before the literature, a grid of what, when and where, and two checks that
catch a gap where a challenged assumption belongs.

None of the source texts ship with the plugin. Every question is the plugin's
own wording, modelled on the structure of its source and citing it with a
resolvable reference; a reader who holds a copy points
`RESEARCH_HARNESS_LIBRARY` at it, and a reader who does not gets the citation.
A test keeps the template parseable, statement-free and aligned with the
fixture's question ids.
