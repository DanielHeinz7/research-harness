title: The guard's refusals show recoveries that work
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

Two refusal texts pointed at commands that do not run as written. When the guard
refuses to accept a statement for you, it now shows the recovery with
`--explained "<your sentence>"`, which `accept` has required since 0.4.0 — the
text without it was followed by a second refusal. And the refusal that keeps the
model from choosing a framing spelled its second comparison command `review
--variant <name>` as if it were a command of its own; it now reads
`research-harness review --variant <name>`. The refusals themselves are
unchanged: accepting and choosing stay the researcher's acts.
