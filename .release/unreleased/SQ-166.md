---
title: An acceptance belongs to the question that was approved
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

`research-harness review` now checks both the question and the text hash when
matching an accepted statement to a verified DECISION. If two questions contain
identical accepted text, a DECISION for one no longer hides the other's missing
approval. Moving an acceptance to another question leaves the original DECISION
stale and the statement at its new location unlogged.
