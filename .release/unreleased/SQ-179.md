title: Two clones' decision logs merge into one sealed chain
bump: minor
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

When two clones both appended after the same entry, the merge takes the other
clone's log byte for byte and re-seals this clone's entries after it: renumbered
from the other side's last number, with `Supersedes` and `Open at acceptance`
references that point at them rewritten through the same map, and a sealed
`MERGE` entry that records what was taken up and where the own entries moved.
Nothing is deleted and no sealed entry is rewritten in place; where one log is a
prefix of the other, the longer one is returned unchanged and no merge entry is
written.

The merge refuses rather than guesses: without the sealing key on this machine,
on a broken chain on either side, on two logs that share no entry at all, and on
two cases that would quietly rewrite the record — an acceptance that would land
after the other side's unanswered check, and a moved entry that points at one
the merge was told to leave out. Each refusal names the entries involved and
writes nothing.
