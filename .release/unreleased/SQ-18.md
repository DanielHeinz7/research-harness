title: The canvas, canvas-review and accept skills
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

The plugin ships its three slash commands. `/research-harness:canvas` works
through the nine elements in dialogue, starting where the mode says —
`brainstorming` at element 1, `seed` through the problematization
sensibilities, `import` by reconstructing the manuscript — and writes only
`[proposed]`, `[tentative]`, and transcriptions of what the researcher actually
said. It leaves an element empty when no answer exists, and it never accepts:
that instruction is a prohibition, with a pointer at the researcher's own
command.

`/research-harness:canvas-review` reads the mechanical report out of the CLI,
then argues against the canvas instead of tidying it — a refutation per
committed element before any report of fit, plus the five contradiction pairs
and the manuscript check. It writes `[proposed]` paragraphs and `DISCOVERY`
entries, nothing else.

`/research-harness:accept` is the human's button. It carries
`disable-model-invocation: true`, so the model cannot invoke it, and a single
`!` line runs the CLI outside the model's tool surface — the write happens
there, which is why the promotion guard does not deny it.

Frontmatter stays at `name` and `description`; every plugin path goes through
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}`, so the long material lives in `references/` and is read
on demand rather than loaded into every invocation.
