ref: SQ-182
title: research-harness merge-log repairs a conflicting pull
bump: minor
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

After a `git pull --no-rebase` that conflicted on `research/decisions.md`,
`research-harness merge-log` takes stage 2 and stage 3 of the file, verifies
both chains, and writes the merged working file: the other clone's log byte for
byte, this clone's entries after the shared part renumbered and re-sealed after
it, and a sealed `MERGE` entry recording where the other side was taken up and
where the own entries moved. The command stages and commits nothing; it re-runs
`verifyChain` on the file it wrote and prints the next steps. Outside a merge,
or during a rebase where ours and theirs are swapped, it refuses and writes
nothing.

An acceptance that would land after the other clone's unanswered check is
refused by name, with the one-step way out: `merge-log --skip-decision <n>`
merges without that acceptance — the entry stays in git history — and the
command says that the statement's marker in `research/canvas.md` may still read
[accepted] and must be set back by hand before accepting again. Entry numbers
are given as `12` or `#12`; a shell drops an unquoted `#12` as a comment, and
that bare flag is refused before anything runs.
