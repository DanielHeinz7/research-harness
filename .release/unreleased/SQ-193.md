---
ref: SQ-193
title: merge-log refuses a copy whose seals do not verify
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

`research-harness merge-log` now refuses, and writes nothing, when the other
clone's `decisions.md` does not verify under the project key. Before, only a
copy that read as broken was refused. A copy without any seal reads as
unverifiable instead, so on a log whose first entries predate the seal, a
pushed copy could drop a seal, put an unsealed acceptance in place of a sealed
entry, or rewrite the header or a text hash, and the merge sealed our entries
on top of it into a log that verified. The shared part now also compares the
text above #1 and every entry without a seal byte for byte, text hash included.

A `Supersedes` or `Open at acceptance` reference on a continuation line of its
field is renumbered with the entry it names, like one on the label line.
`--skip-decision=5` and a repeated `--skip-decision` are refused (exit 2)
instead of being ignored or reduced to the last value.
