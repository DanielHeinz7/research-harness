---
ref: SQ-20
title: A committed statement that no reader would see is reported
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

A statement can be in the canvas and invisible in it. Wrapping one in an HTML
comment, with the comment markers on their own lines, leaves its element,
question, marker, text and tags untouched — so it is still a commitment, the
guard still protects it, and eleven bytes later nobody reading the rendered
canvas can see it. Indenting the same statement by four spaces does the same
thing through a different door.

The parser stays blind to comments, which was the decision to make and not an
oversight. Stripping them would close the comment door, leave the indentation
door open, and put a statement the researcher commented out themselves beyond
the guard's reach, where the model could then change it freely. What the
harness owes them instead is the truth that the file and the eye disagree.

`canvas-review` now reports committed statements that would not render, naming
the statement, its line and which construct hides it, and the session summary
carries the question ids on one line. Proposals are not reported: a `[proposed]`
statement in a comment is the model parking its own suggestion.

Nothing is enforced here and nothing is repaired. A comment is also how a
researcher parks something they are not ready to delete, so which of the two
views is the one they meant is their judgment. The guard's verdict is unchanged
and a test now records that: hiding a committed statement is permitted, and the
review is what makes it visible again.
