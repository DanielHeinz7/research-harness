ref: SQ-203
title: The researcher accepts a glossary entry, and the model cannot
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

`research-harness glossary accept <id> --explained "<sentence>"` marks the
entry `[accepted]` and appends a sealed `DECISION` named `glossary <id>
accepted`, carrying the payload hash the acceptance binds and the accepted
payload in its body. The displayed payload is revalidated against the file
right before the write, so an entry that changed in between refuses as stale
and nothing is written. Element 4's profile dependencies gate the acceptance,
the explanation must be the researcher's own sentence, and an incomplete
entry, an origin that cannot carry an acceptance, an unmet dependency, a
missing key or a broken log each refuse by name before the marker is written —
a refused accept leaves neither a marker without a decision nor a decision
without its text. Reflow does not break the binding; a wording, alias, origin
or distinction change makes the acceptance stale, which `review` reports next
to acceptances that have no cover in the log.

Acceptance is the researcher's act, so the guard refuses it to the model on
every route that reaches the other acts the researcher keeps: the shell under
any name, a wrapper, an interpreter's program text, or an unknown tool. A
`[stated]` or `[accepted]` entry is protected like a canvas commitment: the
model may add or rewrite a `[proposed]` entry and transcribe a new `[stated]`
one, and may not alter, retitle, relink, detag, relabel, rename or remove a
committed one; the glossary's frontmatter is the researcher's. The new
`skills/glossary-accept/SKILL.md` runs the command outside the model and grants
it no tools, and the session protocol renders `glossary <id> accepted` as the
accepted payload rather than as a canvas question.
