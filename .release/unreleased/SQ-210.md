ref: SQ-210
title: Accept glossary definitions with a displayed payload hash
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

The researcher can accept a complete glossary entry as a settled definition.
`research-harness glossary show <id>` prints its canonical payload and
 eight-hex hash without changing state. After reading it,
`research-harness glossary accept <id> --hash <shown hash> --explained
"<sentence>"` marks the entry `[accepted]` and appends a sealed `DECISION`
named `glossary <id> accepted`, carrying the payload hash and canonical
payload. The hash is required on the public acceptance route: a proposal
rewritten since display refuses with both files unchanged. The human-only
`glossary-accept` skill documents this show-then-accept sequence and grants
no model tools.

Element 4's profile dependencies gate acceptance. The explanation must be
the researcher's own sentence; incomplete entries, unresolved origins,
missing keys and broken logs refuse. Synchronous write failures restore the
original glossary and remove any partial ledger append, leaving neither a
promoted marker without a decision nor a decision without its accepted text.
This recovery does not promise cross-process or crash atomicity.

Payload binding includes the id, term, aliases, definition, origin and
distinctions, excluding only the marker. Reflow preserves the binding;
changed fields make acceptance stale. Review reports stale and unlogged
glossary acceptances separately from canvas decisions, and session protocol
renders their accepted payloads. Acceptance changes no canvas marker.

The guard permits literal read-only `glossary show` commands and denies
model `glossary accept` commands, including named wrappers and inline
programs. Committed glossary entries and frontmatter are protected through
the existing state-path and commitment comparison boundaries, including
variants and filesystem aliases; proposals remain editable. The separate
opaque-script authority seam is not changed by this feature.
