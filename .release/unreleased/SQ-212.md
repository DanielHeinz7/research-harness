title: Harden glossary acceptance binding and recovery
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness

Glossary acceptance now requires the full SHA-256 binding digest printed by `glossary show`, while ledger identifiers remain eight hexadecimal digits. Glossary updates use atomic rename, and failure recovery removes owned ledger bytes before restoring the glossary without overwriting detected concurrent changes. Review lists accepted markers without a verified decision as unlogged; the acceptance skill documents the crash window between glossary publication and ledger append.
