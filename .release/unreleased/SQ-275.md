title: Update line in the app shell
bump: patch
plugins:

The desktop app's shell page can show where an app update stands: a "This app" section in the sidebar and a line on the start page say that a version is downloading, ready, or that the check failed and why, and a Restart now button appears once an update is ready. The line stays hidden until the app reports an update, and a browser tab never shows it.
