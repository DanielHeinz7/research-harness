title: Separate project actions and clarify the session view
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

The first-start screen names Placet and keeps its three ways into a project. The sidebar groups Open, Clone and New separately from the current project, explains Send my changes, and keeps only the latest outcome in each group. To talk about has the same name in the sidebar and session view, with a visible outcome and a hint that the unanswered-question count expands. Fields disappear on To talk about and History; Contributions stays on one line, and empty fields keep their dashed borders with stronger ink. New and Clone show folder-name rules only after an invalid name. Export minutes explains the text-file format. Dark dialogs have a distinct surface, and unchecked radio buttons are hollow while keeping their keyboard behavior.
