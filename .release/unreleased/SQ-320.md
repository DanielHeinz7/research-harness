ref: SQ-320
title: The agent panel keeps its way back whole, fits a 280 px column, and names what it removes
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

In the desktop app's agent panel, "Try again" after the guard went off stays one
readable word group down to a 280 px column, and an earlier "Try again" is
switched off once a newer one appears. The header is one line (the guard has its
own line above the rule), the quick buttons sit in two columns and are disabled,
and announced as disabled, whenever Send is, and Send stays on the first screen
of an empty thread. Removing the connection asks "Remove the saved key?" and the
button says "Remove key". A refused connect field gets its message right under
it. The panel now sets its text in Source Serif 4 and Hanken Grotesk from the
fonts that ship with the plugin; the page's policy allows `font-src 'self'` and
nothing else.
