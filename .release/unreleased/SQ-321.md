---
ref: SQ-321
title: Dialogs in the desktop app are no longer hidden under the agent column
bump: patch
plugins:
  - research-harness
---

In the desktop app, a dialog such as New project or Share with co-authors was
centred on the whole window, and the agent column, which the app draws on top
of the page, covered its right part: hints were cut off and the Create or Share
button showed only its first letters. While a dialog is open the column now
steps aside, and it comes back when the dialog closes by Cancel, Escape or
submit. A conversation in the column keeps running meanwhile.
