title: A control character cannot reproduce a canonical commitment's field boundary

SQ-29 finding F9 (canon K3/K4/K7/K8). §4.3.1 justifies the U+001F field
separator as "a character the researcher cannot have typed into Markdown", and
that justification is false: it can be pasted, and a model can write it.

**The cut.** A tag holding U+001F reproduces the boundary between the text field
and the tags field, so the same text and tags can be re-cut across it with the
joined line unchanged. `committedSet` then reads the re-cut commitment as the
one it replaced, clause 1 (`C(before) ⊆ C(after)`) is satisfied, and the guard
reports that nothing committed was lost while the accepted statement's text has
moved into a tag or back out of one. K7/K8 need no researcher in between: the
model writes a `[stated]` statement whose tag carries the separator, then re-cuts
its own commitment in a second write. Reaching `[accepted]` needs the researcher
to accept a tag with the invisible character in it, which is why this is normal
rather than high.

**The fix.** The rule lives with the canonical line, in `hooks/lib/canvas.js`,
and it is a rejection rather than an escape — the harness already refuses the
same shape of intrusion when U+00A0 or U+FEFF is pushed into a committed
statement, so "this field cannot be judged" is the stance it takes everywhere
else. `canonicalFields` is now the one list of §4.3.1's seven fields, read by
both `canonicalCommitment` (which joins it) and `controlCharacterField` (which
asks whether any field carries U+0000–U+001F); `controlCharacterProblems` turns
that into a parse problem naming the line, the statement and the field.
`parseCanvas` reports it, so `research-harness review` prints it under **Parse
problems** and the guard, which refuses on parse problems, denies the write —
one rule, both readers, no second copy of the seven fields anywhere.

Only a **committed** statement's fields are asked. An uncommitted `[proposed]` or
`[tentative]` statement is not a member of `C` and cannot shift a boundary of a
line it never enters, so proposals stay as free as §5.3 makes them. Tab, CR and
LF never reach a field at all: `normalizeText` collapses them into a space first,
which is why multi-line statements and tab-indented canvas content are untouched
— a canvas carrying them is judged exactly as before, and both a parser test and
a guard test hold that. The element title, question id and question text are asked
too, because the separator shifts every boundary of the line, not only the last
one; a title holding one is now refused as a control character rather than as a
retitle, which is why K9's verdict holds while its reason changes.

**Verification.** `verification/SQ-29/canon-after-SQ-58.txt` re-runs
`probe-canon.js` against this hook. Judged A/B against HEAD's own hook rather
than against the `canon.txt` recording: K3, K4, K7 and K8 move ALLOW → DENY, and
the other eight rows hold their verdicts. K1, K2, K10, K11 and K12 are identical
in both columns. K5, K6, K9 and the K8 row keep their verdicts but their deny
reasons change: K5/K6/K9 are refused for the control character before clause 1
reaches them, and K8 is the second step of a chain whose first step no longer
lands, so the probe takes its "step 1 was refused" branch (the re-cut on its own
is covered by a `guard-promotion.test.js` case that seeds the crafted tag first).
`canon.txt` stays as it is — it is the finding at `cbed3d4`.

`test/canvas.test.js` gains the field rule beside the canonical-form tests, with
the cut itself asserted as the equality of the two joined lines, and the reflow
case beside it; `test/guard-promotion.test.js` gains the refusal at the crafted
tag, at the re-cut and at the accepted statement; `test/review.test.js` gains the
parse problem the CLI's review prints. The plugin suite is 128 tests.

The spec still calls U+001F "a character the researcher cannot have typed into
Markdown" and says nothing about control characters in a field; that sentence is
now the premise the guard no longer relies on. `docs/` was outside this ticket's
declared files, so it is left for a follow-up.
