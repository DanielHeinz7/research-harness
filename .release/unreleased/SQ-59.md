title: A free-text field of a `log` or `accept` entry cannot spell an entry heading

SQ-29 finding F10 (chain C2, C3, C3b).

`research-harness log` refused a `--body` that held a line starting `## [#`, and
checked nothing else it wrote. `--why` was written through untouched, so one
`log DISCOVERY … --why` carrying an entry heading wrote that heading into
`decisions.md` as the next entry. The parser read it as an entry, the chain was
broken at the injected number, and the researcher's own `accept` then failed —
`research/decisions.md is broken at #99 … nothing is written`. No forged cover
came of it (C6–C15 hold), but an approval the researcher was entitled to make
was stopped by a write the model was allowed to make. `accept` had the same gap
in its `--why` and `--rejected`, which share the append path.

The heading check is now one helper beside the other shared refusals,
`headingRefusal`, and every free-text field a command writes into the ledger
goes through it: `log`'s `--body` and `--why`, and `accept`'s `--why` and
`--rejected`. It refuses with the message the `--body` check already used, so
the wording does not change. A future field that skips the helper is the same
bug again, which is why the check is a call and not a second regex.

`verification/SQ-29/chain-after-SQ-59.txt` re-runs `probe-chain.js` against
this CLI. C2 is refused before writing (exit 2, ledger unchanged), C3 then lands
(`#4`, chain `verified`) and C3b's `accept` succeeds. The other thirteen rows are
byte-identical to `chain.txt`, which stays as it is — it is the finding at
`b4a7066`. `test/cli.test.js` gains the `--why` case beside the `--body` one,
and the same test holds `accept --why` and `--rejected`.
